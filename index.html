About the Firm

Danson Delavar LLP is a Toronto litigation boutique focused on delivering strategic, sophisticated, and practical advocacy in high stakes disputes. As a team, Tim Danson and Marjan Delavar combine decades of courtroom experience with thoughtful strategic counsel, recognizing that successful advocacy requires not only strength in litigation, but also the judgment to identify risk early, develop effective solutions, and position clients advantageously before disputes escalate.

The practice includes employment law, complex personal injury litigation, complex corporate/commercial/Civil litigation, constitutional litigation, defamation, appellate advocacy, administrative law, sports law, professional negligence, and insurance litigation. As a team, the partners regularly advise and represent corporations, professionals, executives, insurers, and professional athletes in legally and reputationally sensitive matters.

Recognized for meticulous preparation, persuasive advocacy, and a disciplined, results-driven approach to litigation.

As a team, Tim and Marjan's experience spans administrative proceedings, mediations, arbitrations, trials, and appeals before all levels of court, including matters involving significant public scrutiny and complex legal challenges advancing novel and evolving areas of the law.

Whether acting in wrongful dismissal disputes, shareholder and corporate commercial conflicts, libel litigation, regulatory proceedings, or complex civil matters, they approach every case with strategic precision, unwavering attention to detail, and a commitment to achieving practical and effective results for their clients.

Danson Delavar LLP is committed to maintaining a collaborative and responsive relationship with every client. The team places a premium on careful legal analysis, practical advice, and tailored litigation strategies that allow clients to make informed decisions with confidence at every stage of the process.

Their combined depth of experience and disciplined preparation also allows the firm to litigate efficiently and strategically. By identifying the core issues early, anticipating challenges before they arise, and avoiding unnecessary procedural steps, the firm remains focused on advancing its clients' interests in a practical and cost-effective manner — without compromising the quality or strength of the advocacy provided.

Timothy S.B. Danson

Timothy S.B. Danson is a Partner at Danson Delavar LLP. He graduated from Osgoode Hall Law School in 1978 and was called to the Ontario Bar in 1980, after completing the Bar Admission Course at the Law Society of Upper Canada.

Tim maintains a broad litigation and appellate practice spanning corporate/commercial disputes, constitutional, sports law and administrative law, defamation, complex personal injury and medical malpractice, contract, insurance, fraud and fidelity, criminal law, environmental protection, and employment law. He regularly advises and represents corporations, executives, professional athletes, professionals, and government bodies in legally and reputationally sensitive matters. Known for his strategic approach to dispute resolution, Tim works to identify legal risks early, develop creative and discreet solutions, and resolve complex matters through problem identification and consensus building — at trial when necessary, and outside the courtroom whenever possible.

Drawing on 46 years of trial and appellate experience, Tim is recognized for his meticulous attention to detail and preparation, persuasive advocacy, and ability to argue precedent-setting cases at every level of court. His practice has spanned the representation of publicly-traded corporations, individual executives, professional athletes, families of victims of crime, professional associations, and regulators — providing him with an exceptional understanding of litigation strategy, public-interest advocacy, and the dynamics of high-stakes and high-profile proceedings across an exceptionally broad range of complex matters.

Tim has worked extensively on high profile cases including cases involving professional athletes, doping and disciplinary proceedings, and matters attracting significant public scrutiny. He has represented clients before numerous specialized and administrative tribunals, including the Court of Arbitration for Sport, the International Equestrian Federation, and a wide range of professional regulatory bodies.

An experienced trial and appellate advocate, Tim has appeared before all levels of court in Ontario — including many appearances before the Court of Appeal for Ontario and the Supreme Court of Canada.

Advisory Board — Trudeau Centre for Peace, Conflict and Justice at University of Toronto, Munk School of Global Affairs

Outside his litigation practice, Tim is a frequent speaker at judicial conferences, Crown counsel forums, police and medical conferences, and at law schools across Canada and the United States — including Osgoode Hall, Queen's, the University of Calgary, the University of New Brunswick, Western Ontario, and Fordham. He is regularly consulted by provincial and federal governments on matters of legislation and policy. He is the CEO of Danson Sports and Entertainment Ltd., an adjunct to his law practice providing non-legal management and consultation services for athletes around the world.

Tim has also gained a unique perspective on problem identification and resolution as a result of being exposed throughout his life to the diversity and challenges of Canadian and international politics, wherein viewing complex problems requires the ability to see issues from many different perspectives. Tim's father, The Honourable Barnett J. Danson, P.C., C.C., LLD., Lég. d'honneur (Fr.), was also his mentor. Mr. Danson Sr. instilled in Tim a strong sense of public service, discipline, hard work and the importance of conducting one's affairs with civility, professionalism, integrity and honour.

Outside the practice of law, Tim is an avid outdoorsman, with a particular love for extreme deep powder skiing, horseback riding, tennis, hockey, and angling.

Marjan Delavar

Marjan Delavar is a Partner at Danson Delavar LLP. She graduated from Osgoode Hall Law School in 2001 and joined the firm in 2002 after completing her articles in the Legal Department of the Toronto Transit Commission. While at Osgoode, she was a member of the winning Goodman and Carr mooting team and volunteered through the school's Community and Legal Aid Services Program.

Prior to law school, Marjan obtained her Honours Bachelor of Arts degree from York University, graduating Cum Laude. In 1993, she was awarded the Governor General of Canada Bronze Medal.

Marjan maintains a broad litigation practice with a particular focus on employment law, complex civil litigation, and high-stakes personal injury matters. She regularly advises employers and management on wrongful dismissal claims, workplace investigations, performance management, progressive discipline, workplace accommodation, return-to-work strategies, and termination and severance issues. Known for her strategic approach to dispute resolution, she works closely with employers to identify legal risks early, develop effective workplace strategies, and navigate complex employment disputes with care and sensitivity.

Drawing on nearly 25 years of litigation experience, Marjan is recognized for her meticulous preparation and strong advocacy. Her background representing both plaintiffs and Canadian and American insurers has provided her with a deep understanding of litigation strategy, risk assessment, and negotiation dynamics across a broad range of complex matters.

Second chair to her partner Tim Danson, Marjan has worked extensively on defamation, appellate, sports law, and professional regulatory matters — including cases involving professional athletes, doping and disciplinary proceedings, and matters attracting significant public scrutiny. She has represented clients before numerous administrative tribunals, including the Financial Services Commission of Ontario (now LAT), the Workplace Safety and Insurance Appeals Tribunal, the International Equestrian Federation, and the Court of Arbitration for Sport.

An experienced trial and appellate advocate, Marjan has appeared before all levels of court in Ontario — including the Ontario Court of Justice, Superior Court of Justice, Divisional Court, and the Court of Appeal for Ontario — as well as the Federal Court and the Supreme Court of Canada.

Founder & President — Neeki Support Foundation (est. 2012)

Outside her litigation practice, Marjan is actively involved in pro bono advocacy and community initiatives. Neeki Support Foundation is a not-for-profit she founded in 2012 to provide social support and interest-free financial assistance to individuals facing immediate hardship.

An avid hot yogi, a floral and interior design enthusiast and frequent traveller, Marjan enjoys exploring new parts of the world. Deeply proud of her Persian heritage, she draws inspiration from its rich culture, poetry, art, and sense of community, influences that continue to shape both her personal life and worldview.
